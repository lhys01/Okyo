Plans, renewal, trials and how billing works

## What you are buying

Okyo launches without paid plans, trials, purchases, or subscription entitlements. These Subscription Terms remain available for future use; Okyo will publish updated terms and store disclosures before offering a paid feature.

## Future billing

No billing or automatic renewal is active at launch. If Okyo later offers a subscription, the plan, price, renewal rules, and billing period shown before purchase will apply.

## Future trials and offers

No free trial or introductory offer is active at launch. Okyo will describe any future offer before purchase and will not advertise one it cannot deliver.

## Payment

No payment is taken at launch. If paid features are introduced later, Apple will process App Store payments and Okyo will not receive or store card details.

## Managing a future subscription

There is no subscription to manage at launch. Future store and in-app guidance will explain how to manage or cancel a paid plan.

## Restoring a future purchase

There are no purchases to restore at launch. If Okyo later offers paid features, the applicable store and in-app guidance will explain restoration.

## Deleting data

Using “Delete my data” erases what Okyo stores on this device. There is no subscription to cancel or refund at launch.

## Future price and plan changes

If Okyo introduces paid plans, it will show the applicable price and plan details before purchase and follow the store’s rules for later changes.
