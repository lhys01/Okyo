## Read this alongside the Privacy Policy

These notes add region-specific detail. They do not all apply to everyone — each section says who it is for. Availability depends on the countries listed in the App Store.

## A note that changes everything below

Most recipe, scan, grocery, cooking and preference data is stored on this device. Okyo supports email-and-password and Google sign-in through Clerk, and supports Apple sign-in when the Apple and Clerk configuration is enabled for the release. Clerk holds account and authentication records, including email addresses and passwords for password accounts, and Okyo’s service processes requests sent for AI features. No subscription entitlement records are collected by Okyo at launch. The in-app controls cover Okyo data on this device and signed-in account deletion; provider records may have to be handled under those providers’ own processes. Provider-controlled records are also subject to the provider’s retention and privacy practices.

## Europe and the United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK you have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Okyo does not use your data for automated decisions with legal effects.

## California and other US states

If you are a resident of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or another US state with a consumer privacy law, you may have rights to know, delete, correct and port your personal information, and to opt out of sale, sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising, and profiling. Okyo does not sell personal information, does not share it for targeted advertising, and does not profile you for those purposes, so there is no opt-out to offer — if that ever changes, an opt-out will be added before it does. Okyo will not discriminate against you for exercising a right.

## Washington State (My Health My Data)

Dietary restrictions, allergies and nutrition targets can count as consumer health data under Washington law. Okyo stores that information on your device and sends relevant preferences with recipe and AI requests. It does not sell this information or use it for advertising. Contact us about access or deletion.

## Canada, including Quebec

If you are in Canada you may have rights of access and correction under PIPEDA, and Quebec’s Law 25 adds further rights including portability. Contact us to exercise applicable privacy rights.

## Australia

If you are in Australia, the Australian Privacy Principles may give you rights of access and correction, and the Australian Consumer Law gives you consumer guarantees that cannot be excluded. Contact us about applicable access or correction rights.

## How to raise a request

See “Privacy requests and deletion” or contact Okyo at support.okyo@gmail.com. For account and service data, Okyo will need to verify the request using the available account and service records. Records controlled by Apple, Clerk or the AI provider may also be subject to those providers’ request processes. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.
