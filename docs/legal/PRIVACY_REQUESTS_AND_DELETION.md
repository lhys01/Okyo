How to see, correct, export or delete your data

## Access — you already have it

You can view your local profile and goal in Settings, preferences in Dietary preferences and Nutrition targets, recipes in Liked, lists in Grocery, and totals in Stats & progress. Account providers and Okyo’s service also process the information described in the Privacy Policy. Contact support for access to information held by Okyo.

## Correction

Edit anything directly: your name and fox name, your goal, your dietary preferences and nutrition targets, and any generated recipe. A wrong dish result can be corrected in place without rescanning.

## Deletion

Settings › Privacy & data › Delete all my data removes every Okyo record on this device: profile and fox name, goal and onboarding answers, dietary preferences and nutrition targets, saved and generated recipes, grocery lists and checked items, Cook Mode progress, completed meals, savings and macro totals, notification preferences, your recorded permission choices, and the stored scan images. A signed-in user can also choose Delete account from that screen to remove the Clerk account record. The app prevents duplicate account-deletion requests while a request is in progress.

Okyo has no purchase records to remove at launch. Uninstalling Okyo also removes the app’s stored data.

## Export

For a copy of information held by Okyo, contact the support email in this policy. We may need to verify your identity. Provider-controlled records may require a request to that provider.

## Objection and consent withdrawal

You can turn notifications, camera access and photo access off in your device Settings, or turn notification categories off inside Okyo. Analytics is controlled by the publisher’s build configuration, not by an in-app toggle; it sends events only when the PostHog integration is enabled. Okyo does not use analytics for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## Opting out of sale or targeted advertising

There is nothing to opt out of. Okyo does not sell personal information and does not share it for targeted advertising.

## If you would rather talk to a person

For privacy questions, email Okyo at support.okyo@gmail.com. Signed-in accounts can be deleted in the app. A person requesting help with a third-party account record may need to contact that provider directly.
